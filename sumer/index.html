How to play

Sumer is a walk through a 3D copy of Limassol in Cyprus, built from the real map: 31,500 buildings in their real places, every street and path, the parks, beaches, piers and the marina. Explore, swim in the sea, and find the famous places. The orange arrow at the top points to the next one.

LookClick the game, then move the mouse · phone: drag on the right side
WalkWASD or arrows · phone: the round stick
Runhold Shift · phone: 🏃
JumpSpace · phone: ⤒
MapM: jump to any place in the city
ViewC: your eyes / behind you
DroneF: fly over the city (E up, Q down)
TimeT: morning, midday, sunset, night
MenuEsc · H hide the screen buttons
Gamepadsticks to walk and look, A jump, B run, Y view, X map

Buildings, streets, trees and beaches come from OpenStreetMap. Most heights and colours are estimated from the type and size of each building, so a few houses will not look exactly like the real ones.
